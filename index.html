No. It only updates your command line aliases. It leaves your system files alone.

Can I undo the changes?

Yes. You can uninstall the tool through your Windows settings menu. Your terminal returns to its original state.

Is my data secure?

This tool runs locally on your machine. It does not send your data to outside servers beyond what the Claude CLI already performs.
